02 / The sketchbook

Everything starts
with an idea.

Small, useful things I could build while learning the web.

The possibilities

Five things I might build

  • A campus starter guide

    A friendly map of everyday student life: study spots, food, and useful services.

  • A bilingual quote notebook

    A place to collect favourite sentences and compare their meaning across languages.

  • A study session planner

    A simple weekly plan that turns big assignments into manageable sessions.

  • A hometown food journal

    Stories, photographs, and a few recipes behind familiar dishes.

  • A student book exchange

    A small catalogue connecting readers who want to swap used course books.

People first

Who each one is for

Campus starter guide
First-year students finding their way around campus.
Bilingual quote notebook
Chinese and English learners who enjoy reading in both languages.
Study session planner
Students balancing several assignments and deadlines.
Hometown food journal
Students away from home who want to cook familiar meals.
Student book exchange
Students looking for affordable textbooks and less waste.

From thought to browser

How I will build it

  1. Sketch the pages and choose the content.
  2. Write semantic HTML and connect the pages.
  3. Add a shared stylesheet and mobile layouts.
  4. Add JavaScript, one useful interaction at a time.
  5. Test links, keyboard controls, and different screen sizes.
