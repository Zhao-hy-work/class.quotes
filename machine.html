One switch. One very determined little machine.

THE USELESS MACHINE / No. 01

That is all it does.

Tap the switch, or focus it and press Enter or Space.

What is a useless machine?

A box with one switch.

A tiny experiment in cause and effect. Its only job is to undo what you just did.

  1. You flip the switch on.
  2. The lid opens. A hand comes out.
  3. It flips the switch off.
  4. The hand goes back. The lid closes.
